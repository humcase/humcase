# Humcase features

The full list of what Humcase does today. Short presentation: [README](../README.md).

- **Canvas of cases with rich contents**: cases placed freely on a canvas, sub-cases to any depth, and inside a case drawings made with a built-in Excalidraw editor, images and links next to the text. A board goes out to and comes in from JSON Canvas files (`.canvas`, an open format read by other note-taking and whiteboard apps).
- **Cases linked to tasks**: from a thought, create a linked task or attach one from the same project; open the source from the task and follow its progress from the thought. A link can be removed without deleting the cases. Reports can also be written in the application, with a draft that can be recovered if a write fails.
- **Tracking and reports**: choose To do, In progress or Done in the editor. Saved reports, notably those written through MCP, are read in a collapsible Markdown panel. Reopening a case keeps its previous report, and a write that does not have the expected version is refused instead of overwriting a more recent text. The status does not prove that an agent is currently executing a task.
- **Agent editing (MCP)**: in Settings → Agent, explicitly allow an agent connected through MCP to edit the title, text, type, tags, priority and due date of cases. Each modification within this scope is timestamped and attributed to the agent, the previous version is kept and can be viewed in the case's "Agent history" panel, and a write on an outdated version is refused. The raw dictation is not editable and agents cannot delete cases. Status changes and reports go through dedicated tools, independent of this setting; the `set_due_date` tool can also change the due date without this authorization, which protects only `update_note` calls.
- **Sorting cases**: in Cards and in List, "To do" comes first by default, before "In progress" and "Done", then priorities and recent modifications order the cases. The "Sort" control also offers recency, priority or title. "Today" keeps the urgency order and the Board keeps its columns.
- **One filter bar for every view**: search (with `#tag`), status, nature, sub-cases and "Today" sit above the canvas, the cards, the list and the board alike; a filter chosen in one view stays visible in the others, and on the canvas it fades the other cases instead of moving them. "Back" in a case returns to the page it was opened from.
- **Structuring a thought**: from a written or dictated case, "Structure" proposes an editable layout before applying it, following a frame of your choice (automatic, reflection summary, development, marketing, research). The previous version joins the case history ("Restore this version") and an Undo button follows the application; if the case changes in the meantime, the application is refused. The configured provider receives only this text and the space/project/areas context, without the other cases. This assistance is optional; check the facts before confirming.
- **Spaces and areas**: choose Personal, Work or both in Settings → Spaces, and enable a combined view if you wish. Each project keeps its space; projects that have none stay in "Unsorted" until you file them. Add areas (Development, Marketing, Travel, Outings or your own) and file a project under several areas of its space; removing an area keeps the projects and their cases. These settings organize the interface and do not restrict MCP access.
- **Projects**: a project's "…" button lets you rename it, change its color, archive it (hidden, nothing is erased) or delete it (its cases go to the Inbox). A case can also be archived from its page; "Archived" in the cases view shows archived cases again.
- **Inbox**: capture a thought without choosing a project, in Work or Personal, then file it in a project of the same space. Quick capture also offers this destination. This filing works without AI and is part of the archives.
- **Trash**: a deleted case stays 30 days in the trash (sidebar), with its links, history and images, and comes back identical with "Restore" or "Undo" right after deletion. After that it is erased when the application starts; "Empty trash" and erasing a single case ask for confirmation. Archives include the trash.
- **Settings**: six sections in a full-window view, General (language, theme, text size with a preview, spaces), Dictation, AI services (every key in one place, and the usage), Suggestions, Agents and Data.
- **Case page**: title and text share a continuous writing surface. Organization and tracking remain accessible alongside, then below the case in a narrow window. Filing from the Inbox unfolds on demand. Clicking or creating a case opens this editable page directly; the full report and linked tasks are on the same page, and the preserved raw source can be compared with the structured text.
- **Home and cards**: Home opens on your day (what you and your agents wrote, hour by hour), then what agents did since your last visit (results waiting for your decision, writes you have not opened, reworks), then the case to pick up, what to follow and the recent cases; a case shows once, in the highest place it appears. Cards show the title before the excerpt, adapt their height to their content and show tags, branch and an excerpt of the report when they exist; the first local image of a case or of its report can serve as a thumbnail. The List and Board views are also available; the list keeps a bounded reading width on large screens and offers creation next to search.
- **Visual design**: light and dark themes with a neutral palette and an orange accent, Phosphor icons drawn as inline SVG (no connection required), shared by settings, the editor, mini mode and quick capture. Technical reference: [design system](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).
- **Local-first (SQLite)**: no cloud synchronization required, data stored on your machine.
- **Full backup and restore**: from Settings, create a portable `.humcase.zip` archive containing a consistent copy of the database and the linked images, or restore an archive after reviewing it and confirming the replacement. A safety backup of the current state is created before the restore. AI provider keys are not included. Stray files in the `images` folder (invalid or oversized orphans, directories, links) are ignored; an image **referenced** by a case that cannot be read makes the backup fail on purpose.
- **Rich Markdown editor (Tiptap)**: real-time editing with local syntax highlighting (`lowlight`), pasted images, links, tables and checklists preserved on re-edit. `Ctrl+F` in a case searches its text (occurrences highlighted, Enter / Shift+Enter to move between them, Escape to return to the text).
- **Recoverable drafts**: if a save fails, "Retry" compares the current case before replaying the draft's fields. In case of conflict, the text remains displayable so it can be recovered manually.
- **Built-in CLI**: run `Humcase.exe add "Title"` or `Humcase.exe list` from PowerShell; `note`, `relations`, `create-linked-task`, `link-task`, `unlink-task`, `status` and `report` cover tracking a thought through to its result. Every MCP tool is also available from the CLI with the same safeguards: `Humcase.exe tools` lists them and `Humcase.exe call <tool> --args '{"key":"value"}'` runs one (`--args -` reads the JSON from stdin); `handoff` and `projects` are shortcuts. Commands return a non-zero exit code on error. In PowerShell, pipe the output (`| Out-String`): PowerShell does not wait for the app when its output is redirected with `>`.
- **Reminders for agents**: hooks for Claude Code and Codex give the agent the brief at session start and remind it to track and report its work (`docs/AGENT_SETUP.md`).
- **Voice dictation (Deepgram)**: transcription by Deepgram (Nova-3) in the language you speak (the interface language by default, or one chosen in Settings, including several languages in one dictation), cleanup by an LLM of **your choice** (the first one whose key is set, or OpenAI / Groq / OpenRouter / Mistral / Anthropic (Claude) / Ollama (local) / an OpenAI-compatible service). Provider, **model** and **key** are set in Settings; the key is stored on the application side (**encrypted at rest by DPAPI**, never read back by the interface), with a Windows environment variable still possible as a fallback. For a model running on your computer (Ollama, LM Studio or any OpenAI-compatible server), enter its **server address** in Settings; **Test connection** lists the available models without sending any content. A dictation session is **capped at 15 min** (clean shutdown, last fragment included), and a local journal (`dictation-journal.jsonl`, next to the database) keeps what was dictated and what the AI returned. Another local file (`ai-usage.jsonl`) counts what each AI call and dictation consumed, numbers only; **Settings → AI consumption** shows the dictated minutes, AI calls and tokens by use and by model, without prices.
- **Smart dictation** (enabled by default): at the end of the dictation, the whole case is reworked with the dictation integrated, structured and corrected; the previous version goes into the history, with Undo, and the raw dictated text is always kept. When disabled, or if the case changed during processing, the cleaned dictation is appended at the end.
- **Works without AI**: while no AI is connected, **Structure** stays visible but locked with a **Connect an AI** link, and dictation is added as dictated (raw text always kept).
- **Automatic title**: as long as the title has not been typed, it is **derived from the first meaningful line** of the content (manual input as well as dictation) and freezes as soon as you edit the Title field.
- **Built-in MCP server**: any compatible MCP client can launch `Humcase.exe mcp` to read the same projects and notes, create and track linked tasks, build sub-cases with their provenance, and, with the agent editing authorization, ask Humcase to structure a case with the frame of their line of work (`structure_note`). Each client configures its own launch of the server; the data stays shared. The server presents itself under the name "Humcase" and passes its writing guidelines to agents (notes structured like "Structure", linked tasks, status and report). Specification: [MCP_SPEC](MCP_SPEC.md).
- **"Humcase" Claude Code plugin** (`plugins/humcase`): declares the MCP server under the name `humcase` and adds four skills, `resume-work` (reload the brief and the reworks before acting), `write-note` (write a case with the frame of the line of work), `organize-cases` (check for duplicates, file what belongs to one subject inside its case as sub-cases, keep links for cross-references) and `deliver-results` (resolve after verification with a report, handle reworks). Installation: `claude plugin marketplace add humcase/humcase` then `claude plugin install humcase@humcase`; the executable path is requested at activation. The same skills ship for Codex. Setup for Claude Code, the Claude desktop app, Codex, opencode and other clients: [AGENT_SETUP](AGENT_SETUP.md).
- **Search by meaning** (installers): a search also finds the cases that say the same thing in other words or in another language ("Close in meaning", under the search). Humcase reads the cases with its own model, on your computer, in the background; Settings → Search by meaning shows where it stands and turns it off. Agents use `find_by_meaning`.
- **Ask Humcase** ("Demander à Humcase", Ctrl+J): ask a question about your work and Humcase answers from the cases of the space, each sentence with its source; a source shows the case and the sentences it cites, word for word, and opens it. A sentence the AI cannot tie to a case is not shown. A follow-up question keeps the conversation of the session; "Keep as a case" files an answer in the Inbox, and a proposed next step becomes a sub-case only when you click. One call to the AI chosen in Settings (excerpts of the closest cases and one line for each of the 25 latest); without an AI, the closest cases. When the first reading is weak, the AI searches the cases again with other words before answering.
- **Think it through** ("Réfléchir avec Humcase"): the second mode of the panel, or the button in a case. Humcase restates, links what your cases already say with their sentence, proposes a lead when you hesitate and asks one question at a time; you can dictate your answers. At the end, a summary to correct and keep in the case, with the whole conversation; ticked actions become sub-cases. Nothing is written before you keep it.
- **Sprout** ("Faire germer"): in a case, Humcase proposes its next steps as sub-cases, each quoting the passage it comes from; you check, correct or refuse, nothing is created without you. One call to the AI chosen in Settings; agents propose in the same panel (`propose_sub_cases`).
- **How you work**: a work profile per space that you edit freely and that every agent reads at the start of a session; Humcase proposes lines for it (decisions that are working rules, your lines of work, what you often refuse) and you choose. On Home, a few questions, ideas and decisions waiting for a next step, once a day.
- **To tidy** ("À ranger"): on Home, Humcase proposes to archive cases resolved long ago, to mark the decisions a newer decision replaces, to file close cases together and points out the cases that may go against a decision in force; you answer, nothing is deleted, and a replaced decision stays readable as history for you and for agents.
- **Project summary**: a project's decisions in force, work in progress, open questions and what to check, on one page and for agents (`get_project_summary`).
- **Natures to check** (optional, TypeSafe key): Jev, TypeSafe's model, reads your cases again and Humcase proposes another nature when one fits better, one case at a time from Home; nothing changes without you. Off until turned on in Settings → Suggestions, with the TypeSafe key in Settings → AI services; the title and first 300 words of each case are sent, without keys or email addresses.
- **Similar cases**: SQLite's FTS5 search suggests close cases to help spot duplicates. Suggestions are not exhaustive and do not block creation.
- **Board, quick capture and mini mode**: Board view by status (drag and drop), global quick capture `Alt+Shift+N` (to a project, the Inbox or the open case, whose draft receives the text appended), pinned mini mode to keep a case in view.
- **Due dates and handoff**: due date by day, "Today" (the cases in progress, overdue, due today or of high priority across the whole space, sub-cases included, narrowing any view like a filter) and a `get_handoff` resume brief assembled from the database for agents.
- **Single window**: Humcase opens only one window; relaunching it brings the already open one to the front (even if hidden in the notification area). The MCP server is not limited.

## Restoring from an archive

1. In Settings, paste the full path of a `.humcase.zip` archive, then click
   **Check the archive**. Check the date, the schema and the number of projects, cases and images.
2. Click **Restore this archive**, read the warning, tick the confirmation, then
   click **Confirm full replacement**.
3. The restore replaces the active projects and cases and puts the archive's images back in place.
   A safety archive of the present data is created automatically before the replacement.
   Old image files stay on disk as a precaution, even if they are no longer linked to the
   restored cases.
4. Before confirming, close the other Humcase sessions, including MCP servers and the CLI. The
   restore waits for the editor's changes to be saved; send or
   save elsewhere any Quick Capture draft, then empty it before starting again.
   The application restarts after a successful restore. If an incident occurs during the
   replacement (failure during publication or lost session lock), the application stays in
   read-only mode: quit it from its icon in the notification area (Quit) then relaunch it;
   a safety archive lets you go back.

Local AI keys remain stored separately. They are neither imported nor replaced by the
restore of an archive.
